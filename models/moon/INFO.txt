Moon_albedo.png  (2048×1024)
  From SVS `lroc_color_poles_4k.tif` (LROC WAC color + LDAM polar fill).
  Direct download: …/a004720/lroc_color_poles_4k.tif

Moon_bump.png / Moon_normal.png
  Height from SVS `ldem_16_uint.tif` (LOLA gridded DEM, 16 px/deg).
  Bump 0..1 ↔ ~12.3 km relief (p0.5..p99.5), ≈0.71% of R=1737.4 km — OK vs circumference.
  Normal map: tangent-space from height with strength ≈ span/(2·texel_m) (~1.16),
  matched to moon radius (was ~8 ≈ 7× exaggerated).
  Direct download: …/a004720/ldem_16_uint.tif
  Note: engine HEIGHT shader path is UV/screen-space, not radius-scaled.
  Underlying LOLA products also archived on NASA PDS (public domain).

Moon_specular.png
  Soft regolith specular authored locally from the PD albedo (+ slight bump
  flattening): darker maria slightly higher specular, highlands more matte.
  Neutral grayscale (no cool water / ice chroma) so Earth sunglint / ice-albedo
  shader terms do not fire. Not a NASA specular product.

Note: Curated downloads on the LROC ASU website (non-PDS) can be copyrighted.
These bundled maps come from NASA SVS / PDS-derived kit files, not those
copyrighted web downloads.

Use: enable Diffuse + Specular + Specular-Map + Bump + Normal maps.

Shots: shots/01.png … 10.png — emulator orbit stills (Vulkan SceneViewer).

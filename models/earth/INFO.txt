Earth texture sources (bundled with Earth.obj)
==============================================
Formats: Earth.obj + Earth.mtl, Earth.3DS

Earth_albedo.png
  Based on https://github.com/turban/webgl-earth images/2_no_clouds_4k.jpg
  (NASA Blue Marble derived; common in open WebGL earth demos)

Earth_bump.png
  Height/relief derived from NASA Earth Observatory Blue Marble
  world.topo.bathy.200412.3x5400x2700.jpg (public domain NASA imagery)
  https://visibleearth.nasa.gov / eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov

Earth_normal.png
  mrdoob/three.js examples/textures/planets/earth_normal_2048.jpg (MIT)
  https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js

Earth_specular.png
  Base ocean mask: three.js earth_specular_2048.jpg (MIT), then authored
  layers from albedo + bump:
    - water: strong cool specular (open ocean)
    - ice/snow: bright near-white specular (polar ice, sea ice, mountain snow);
      deserts excluded via warm chroma (R>B)
    - land: near-matte
  Multiplies MTL Ks under key/sun light (Blinn-Phong). Cool-bright water
  texels also drive the shader sunglint term (sharp Fresnel streak).
  Neutral-bright ice/snow texels drive the high-albedo polar glow (broad
  diffuse return, not a mirror point).

Use: open Earth.obj in the file browser; enable Diffuse + Specular + Specular-Map
+ Bump + Normal maps. Rotate so the key light glances off oceans (sunglint)
and lights the polar caps (dazzling white albedo).

Preview: preview.png — emulator still (Vulkan SceneViewer).

BusterDrone — glTF sample (links only; no mesh in this pack)
============================================================
Formats in upstream: scene.gltf + scene.bin + textures/
  (Filament ships Draco-compressed glTF; Assimp needs ASSIMP_BUILD_DRACO
   or a Draco-decoded copy.)

This playground folder does **not** redistribute the model files (license /
size). Get them from Google Filament or Sketchfab — see links below.

Geometry / materials (when loaded)
  Animated hard-surface drone (legs, turbines, blades, panels, emissive eye).
  PBR metallic-roughness: baseColor, metallicRoughness (G=rough, B=metal),
  normals, emissive maps. Rest pose works without animation; spinning
  propellers / actuators need glTF animation playback (not in the stills).

Use with Assimp / PingPong2026Assimp
  - Enable glTF importer + Draco (or decode with gltf-transform / similar).
  - Bake node transforms (`aiProcess_PreTransformVertices`) so turbines/blades
    sit in world space.
  - Shade metallic-roughness (Cook-Torrance), not Blinn-only — baseColor + MR
    + emissive maps.
  - Keep `scene.bin` and `textures/` next to `scene.gltf`.
